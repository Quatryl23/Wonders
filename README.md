# The Seven Wonders of the Ancient World in Minecraft
- Seven new, rare structures of impressive size
- Special challenges, including minigames and bossfights
- Unique items with new abilities

*The structures are built to match the true size and (mostly) the appearance of their historical counterparts. However, the interiors are often more vaguely adopted to make the exploration more interesting and add fun gameplay.*

## The Hanging Gardens of Babylon
<img width="960" height="540" alt="gardens" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/188dbbcd-4adb-4b71-8f63-2e9a88e31d1e" />

This terrace complex is said to contain more plants than the Nether and End dimension combined (probably way more)! And it's also the most unwieldy place in the desert, so why not play a game of Hide and Seek there?

<details>
  <summary><strong>Challenge</strong></summary>
  Meet the villager at the top and play Hide and Seek (Find them five times!)
  <br>
  <br>
  <details>
    <summary><strong>Dangers</strong></summary>
    
  - Wasted time
  </details>
</details>
<details>
  <summary><strong>Loot</strong></summary>
  
  - Plants of all kinds
  - Gardening tools
  - Ancient Debris
  <details>
    <summary><strong>Hoe of the Hanging Gardens
    </strong></summary>
    
  - Highly durable diamond hoe
  - Farmland tilled with the Hoe of the Hanging Gardens will cause improved plant growth in a 9x9 block area:
    - Crops will spread to adjacent open farmland
    - Crops will overgrow adjacent crops with a slightly lower stage of growth
  - Tilling farmland will cause other affected farmland up to 100 blocks away to stop their improved plant growth
  </details>
</details>

## The Mausoleum of Halicarnassus

  </details>
</details>

  - Bones
  - Ancient Debris
  - Damaged military equipment
  <details>
